THE DESIGNER

Rafiu Nafiu Olaide

I am Rafiu Nafiu Olaide, a graphic designer and multidisciplinary creative focused on brand identity, product advertising, film publicity and social media visuals. I develop concepts, direct visual presentation, refine compositions and select final work with attention to brand consistency. My process uses digital design techniques and AI-assisted creative workflows where appropriate. I completed the University of Ilorin U-INSPIRE Graphic Design and Leadership programme.

Creative direction meets considered execution.
